Opening

Recruitment

Research openings for highly motivated students

Our group is currently recruiting students for 2027 admission, including master-to-Ph.D. students, regular Ph.D. students (application-assessment track), and master's students. Research internships are also welcome.

Advantages of Our Research Group

Abundant Resources and Comprehensive Guidance

Our research group is growing rapidly and has abundant computing resources and research funding to support your work. We place great emphasis on the development of every student.

The supervisor provides hands-on mentorship for every new group member, helping them read papers, debug code, and write research articles. We are committed to thoroughly revising each student's first research paper from beginning to end.

Industry-Relevant Research Topics

The research topics in our group are closely aligned with problems of interest to the industry. For students aiming to pursue careers in the industry, such research experience will provide a significant advantage.

Our group encourages students who meet the graduation requirements to undertake research-based internships in companies. We have established in-depth collaborations with leading companies such as ByteDance, Huawei, Baidu, and SenseTime. Outstanding graduates may also receive recommendations to these companies or for doctoral/postdoctoral opportunities at institutions such as the University of Hong Kong, CUHK, the University of Sydney, and Shanghai AI Lab.

Expectations for Prospective Students

Strong Self-Motivation

We provide abundant guidance and research resources to all members, and we hope each student can fully leverage these resources with strong self-motivation to deliver truly valuable scientific contributions.

Early Engagement in Research

After securing postgraduate recommendation or admission, students intending to join the group are welcome to work on their undergraduate thesis and other research projects with us during their fourth year. Starting research a year early will greatly benefit your future development. Papers published during this period can count toward the output requirements for graduate school.

Strong Self-Learning and Programming Skills

Students joining the group are expected to be proficient in Python, PyTorch, and basic Linux operations, with considerable programming experience. The following experiences are highly valued: participation and awards in ACM competitions; in-depth involvement in website development, software development, or open-source projects; and long-term, valuable knowledge sharing in professional fields.

Before You Apply

1. Present one of our papers

Select one paper published by our group (see Publications) and prepare a presentation (PPT) that explains its motivation, core method, and main experimental results.

2. Propose your own improvements

In the same PPT, analyze the paper's limitations and propose your own ideas for improvement or future extension.

Application Information

Send your application

Please send your PPT, resume, and academic transcript to jinyangguo@buaa.edu.cn. Applications without the PPT may not be considered. If you are passionate about research, we look forward to having you join us!
