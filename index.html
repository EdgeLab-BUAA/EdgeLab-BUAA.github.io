Our Research

Efficient foundation models and deployment

We develop compression, quantization, sparsification, and knowledge distillation methods for large language models, diffusion models, segmentation models, and vision systems.

Representative publications and benchmarks

Recent work includes LLM compression benchmarks, small language model quantization, medical LLM compression evaluation, post-training quantization, sparsity benchmarks, and efficient model deployment papers.

Join the Lab

We welcome students interested in efficient AI computing, model compression, PyTorch systems, open-source research, and AI4Science. Strong candidates are self-motivated and comfortable with Python, PyTorch, and Linux.
